About AutoCare

AutoCare Service is a fictional modern car service center created for this project. It focuses on reliable vehicle maintenance and a convenient online booking experience, so customers can keep their car in good condition without unnecessary hassle.

Our fictional workshop handles everyday maintenance such as oil changes, brake service, tire service and basic diagnostics, all organized around clear appointment times.

Our Approach

Four principles guide how AutoCare works with every customer.

  • Honest communication — we explain what a vehicle needs before any work begins.
  • Organized service — every appointment follows a clear, repeatable process.
  • Practical maintenance — we focus on the maintenance that actually keeps a car reliable.
  • Customer convenience — booking and scheduling are simple and online.

How We Handle a Service Request

Every booking follows the same six-step process.

  1. Customer chooses a service.
  2. Customer selects a convenient date and time.
  3. The service request is reviewed.
  4. The vehicle is inspected.
  5. The service is completed.
  6. The customer receives an update.
